A4 is 210 × 297 mm. US Letter is 215.9 × 279.4 mm (8.5 × 11 inches). Letter is a little wider and noticeably shorter. That's enough to push your last line onto a second page.

## Which one to use

| Where you're applying | Paper size |
|---|---|
| United States, Canada, Mexico, the Philippines | US Letter |
| Everywhere else: UK, Europe, the Gulf, India, Pakistan, Africa, Australia, Asia | A4 |

If you apply to both, keep a version in each size. Most recruiters will never print your resume, but some do, and a PDF in the wrong size prints with odd margins or a cut-off edge.

US Legal (8.5 × 14 inches) is for legal documents, not resumes. Kitwise Resume offers it for the rare cases where it's requested.

## Why switching can break a layout

Because Letter is 18 mm shorter, a resume that just fits on one A4 page often spills onto a second Letter page, leaving a lonely section at the top of page 2. Going the other way, from Letter to A4, can leave empty space at the bottom.

## Switching safely in Kitwise Resume

1. Choose **A4**, **US Letter** or **US Legal** above the preview. The pages re-flow straight away, and every new page starts with proper space at the top.
2. If a short resume now runs onto a second page, tick **Fit to one page**. It shrinks the text slightly (never below a readable size) until everything fits.
3. Still too long? Tighten **Spacing** in **Design**, or trim an older role.

To keep both versions, open **My resumes → Duplicate this resume**, then set one copy to A4 and the other to US Letter.
